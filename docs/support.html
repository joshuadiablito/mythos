Questions

Why can I only read one story a day?

Stories arrive one per day for each language, so there is always something new to read. Every story that has arrived stays in Stories. Learning more than one language gives you a story in each.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are on in Mythos's Settings tab and in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Mythos. Each language has its own delivery time.

A word says "Not in the dictionary".

The dictionary is Wiktionary, which doesn't have a page for every form of every word. Mythos offers Apple's on-device translation instead, and the story's own glossary and English translation still work.

Looking up words doesn't work.

The first lookup of a word needs an internet connection. After that it's saved on your phone. Stories, translations and read-aloud all work offline.

The voice sounds robotic.

Mythos uses your iPhone's voices. For a better one, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, choose the language and download an Enhanced or Premium voice.

How do I change language or level?

In Mythos's Settings tab you can add or remove languages, change levels and set the time for each.
