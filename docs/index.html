Mythos

Learn a language one short story at a time.

Every day Mythos gives you a new story, written for your level, in the language you're learning. Tap any word to see what it means, who is doing it and every form it takes.

Seven languages, six levels

French, Spanish, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese and Greek, from A1 to C2: 630 original stories, all in the app.

Read, listen, remember

  • Conjugation and declension tables, with the English under every form
  • Stories read aloud, each word highlighted as it's spoken
  • An English translation, sentence by sentence
  • A notification when each day's story arrives, at the time you choose
  • Saved words reviewed as flashcards, and a reading streak

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.
