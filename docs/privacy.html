Mythos

Privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Mythos doesn't collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Mythos has no server of its own.

What stays on your phone

Your languages, levels, notification times, reading progress, streak and saved words are stored on your device only. They are included in your device's own backups, as with any app, and are deleted when you delete Mythos.

Looking up words

When you tap a word, Mythos fetches that word's dictionary entry from kaikki.org, a free dictionary built from Wiktionary, and may play a pronunciation recording hosted by Wikimedia. These requests contain only the word and the language. Like any website, those services receive your device's IP address in order to answer, and handle it under their own policies. Mythos keeps a copy of each entry on your device so the same word isn't requested twice.

Everything else happens on the device

  • Stories and translations are built into the app.
  • Reading aloud uses your iPhone's own voices.
  • Translating a word uses Apple's on-device Translation.
  • Notifications are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server.

Children

Mythos collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
